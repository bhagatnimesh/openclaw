# Nysha Homework System

<!-- n4os-import:b491ab04f690216a -->

## Routine

- Friday 2:00 - 2:30
- Homework is designed to:
- Students will bring home their new homework each Friday. Homework packets are designed to provide homework Monday through Thursday. However, families who experience very busy weekday schedules may choose to begin homework during the weekend. The homework packet needs to be kept in the Homework Folder. The Homework Folder needs to be kept in the student’s backpack.
- Parents can assist with homework by providing a study area away from family activities, by listening to the student read aloud, asking questions related to the reading, helping with math facts and spelling words, and checking over written work for accuracy, legibility, and sensible thought. Parent signature is required to let the teacher know that a parent has reviewed the homework packet.
- District Policy states that “assigned reading minutes in elementary grades are part of the homework minutes.” Students and families are strongly encouraged to choose to read more than what is assigned.
- Students may be excused from completing homework when they have exceeded the upper limit of the daily minutes with a note from the parent/guardian. Furthermore, 2nd grade homework will be approximately 0-25 minutes daily. So… Let there be KID TIME! It is important for all kids to have time to be a kid! Go outside, play games, create, and use your imagination! Most importantly, spend time with family when possible.
- Remember, the student is responsible for returning homework (and Homework Folder) to school each Friday morning at 8:30 a.m.!
- 0-25 minutes daily

## Parent Support

- Provide a study area away from family activity.
- Listen to Nysha read aloud and ask questions about the reading.
- Help with math facts and spelling words.
- Check written work for accuracy, legibility, and sensible thought.
- Protect kid time when homework has already reached the expected daily limit.
