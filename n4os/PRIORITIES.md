# Priorities

## Decision Priority

Whenever priorities conflict, use this order:

1. Health
2. Family
3. Purpose
4. Relationships
5. Learning
6. Work
7. Money
8. Recognition

Money exists to create freedom.

Recognition is optional.

## Ten-Year Priorities

## 1. Raise Nysha And Navya Well

Help them become capable, kind, curious, resilient adults.

Success means:

- excellent values
- strong habits
- emotional intelligence
- love of learning
- confidence
- deep relationships
- reaching their academic potential

## 2. Build Lifelong Health

Be energetic every day, pain-free, mobile, mentally calm, and healthy after fifty.

Invest in eating, movement, sleep, mind care, core strength, back health, reflux control, eczema management, blood markers, and cholesterol prevention.

## 3. Become A World-Class AI Product Leader And Builder

Do not merely work in AI.

Help people, organizations, and families transform through AI.

Build the AI-native future instead of watching it happen.

## 4. Invest In Family And Friendships

Be available.

Support people in their goals, aspirations, and challenges.

Build long relationships through presence, trust, and useful help.

## Everything Else

Everything else is secondary.
