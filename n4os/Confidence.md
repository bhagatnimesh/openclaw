# Confidence

- In N4OS, confidence means each child feels capable, loved, prepared, and willing to try hard things.
- It matters because confidence compounds into learning, friendships, public voice, resilience, and calm action.
- For [[family/Nysha|Nysha]], current observations suggest confidence may vary by setting: easier with kids, harder with adults or new people.
- For [[family/Navya|Navya]], confidence is something to protect through safety, play, curiosity, and emotional expression.
