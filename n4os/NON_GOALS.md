# Non-Goals

I am intentionally not optimizing for:

- promotion
- title
- external prestige
- being liked by everyone
- maximum compensation
- maximum financial return
- career ladder movement
- best rating
- job guarantee
- product success guarantee
- coolest project
- maximum scope
- perfect behavior
- perfect execution
- certainty before acting
- avoiding failure
- avoiding discomfort
- avoiding being misunderstood
- minimum work for maximum output

If these come naturally while pursuing my mission, great.

They are never the objective.

## Useful Reframe

Do the work that builds health, family, purpose, relationships, learning, and long-term freedom.

Let the score take care of itself.
