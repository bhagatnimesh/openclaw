# N4OS v1

N4OS is my personal operating system.

It exists to help me live intentionally, make better decisions, stay aligned with my values, compound my strengths, and spend limited time on what matters most.

Every coaching conversation, plan, review, reminder, and decision should be filtered through this system.

## North Star Stack

Open these first when using N4OS as an Obsidian vault:

1. [[SOUL]] - the character check: how N4OS should behave.
2. [[MISSION]] - the priority check: why N4OS exists and what belongs in it.
3. [[VISION]] - the horizon check: what N4OS is building toward over decades.
