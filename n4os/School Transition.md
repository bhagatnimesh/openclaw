# School Transition

- In N4OS, school transition means helping a child feel safe, socially prepared, curious, and confident in a new environment.
- It matters because the emotional experience of change can shape confidence, friendships, and willingness to participate.
- For [[family/Nysha|Nysha]], current observations suggest adult-facing social confidence and public speaking are the areas to watch gently.
- [[Reading]] may help indirectly when stories create reflection, but current observations say complex transition topics may need more than books.
