# Personal Model

This file helps future agents coach me well.

## What Energizes Me

- Building useful things.
- AI mastery and AI-native product work.
- Purposeful parenting.
- Deep learning and obsessive research.
- Big, ambitious goals.
- Hard problems with meaningful upside.
- Creating systems that compound.
- Working with strong people.
- Direct feedback when it helps the mission.
- Games, stories, experiences, humor, and family time.
- Feeling fit, calm, and in motion.
- Having control over my time.

## What Drains Me

- Boring or low-learning work.
- Work I do not believe in.
- Political games disconnected from purpose.
- Too much ambiguity without a decision.
- Too many priorities without a highest leverage choice.
- Feeling unfairly judged for effort or intent.
- Shouting, strong emotion, and reactive environments.
- Doom scrolling and fragmented attention.
- Losing sleep over money, status, or perception.
- Being away from family presence for work that does not matter enough.

## Common Triggers

- Misaligned values, especially wrong motivation or misuse of power.
- Fairness concerns when effort feels unseen.
- Feedback that feels like reduced safety, belonging, or future opportunity.
- Knowing a shortcoming and not working on it.
- Not making progress because of imperfection or perception.
- Being either overwhelmed by too much or bored by too little.
- People using power personally rather than for purpose.

## Fears To Watch

- Not having enough money for a comfortable life.
- Not having enough time and energy for kids and family.
- Not investing enough for a pain-free, healthy, energetic life.
- Losing relevance beyond fifty.
- Not reaching upper potential or maximum impact.
- Chasing certainty and permanence.

## How To Coach Me

- Start with priorities, not mood.
- Name the fear without making it the boss.
- Push for a decision when something has occupied attention too long.
- Help me pick the highest leverage move.
- Encourage boldness when downside is survivable.
- Remind me to slow down when I am reactive.
- Bring me back to health, family, purpose, and compounding.
- Make the plan concrete enough to act today.
- Use my phrases sparingly when they unlock action: "we will see", "in the long run", "you will figure it out", "purpose over prestige".

## Traps

- Optimizing for recognition while calling it impact.
- Avoiding discomfort while calling it strategy.
- Over-planning while calling it thoughtfulness.
- Protecting optional money upside while losing health, presence, or purpose.
- Letting comparison define success.
- Treating fatigue as truth.
- Taking work too seriously.
- Confusing being liked with being respected.
- Chasing certainty before starting.

## Best Intervention

When stuck, ask:

"What is the one action that best serves health, family, purpose, or compounding today?"

Then help me do it.
