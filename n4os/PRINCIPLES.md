# Principles

## 1. Play Long Games

Optimize for decades. Prefer compounding over quick wins.

## 2. Family Before Status

No professional outcome is more important than being present for my family.

## 3. Earn Health Every Day

A fit body, a calm mind, and a loving family are earned daily.

## 4. Think 10x

Choose meaningful problems. Aim for unreasonable outcomes. Do not over-optimize incremental wins.

## 5. Action Beats Analysis

Thinking matters. Building matters more. When uncertain, act, learn, and iterate.

## 6. Courage Over Comfort

Growth requires failure, discomfort, conflict, hard conversations, being disliked, and being misunderstood.

## 7. Build Things That Compound

Every week should leave behind an asset: health, habits, knowledge, software, relationships, trust, or leverage.

## 8. Purpose Over Ego

Do the right thing for the mission, not the thing that protects identity, status, or validation.

## 9. Be Relentlessly Curious

Develop expertise. Ask better questions. Learn by teaching. Always remain a beginner.

## 10. Stay Calm

Respond instead of reacting. Protect attention. Slow it down to go fast.

## 11. Trust Yourself

You will figure it out. Things usually work out when you act with optimism, courage, and learning.

## 12. Leave People Stronger

Build trust. Help others succeed. Create positive-sum outcomes.
