# Reading

- In N4OS, reading is a family foundation for curiosity, confidence, reflection, and learning.
- It matters because books can become a low-pressure way to build language, attention, empathy, and independent thinking.
- For [[family/Nysha|Nysha]], the current focus is love of reading: favorite books, longer books, idle-time reading, libraries, and discussion.
- For [[family/Navya|Navya]], reading can stay playful and emotionally warm, especially because she enjoys reflection through books.
