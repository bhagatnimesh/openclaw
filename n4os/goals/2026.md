# 2026 Goals

## Theme

Fit, ambitious, optimistic, hardworking, relentless, present, courageous, caring, and decisive.

## Health

- Build strong core, less pain, more energy.
- Reduce back pain.
- Manage reflux and eczema.
- Keep blood markers strong and cholesterol risk low.
- Protect sleep and recovery.
- Create a daily movement baseline.

## Family

- Build a house full of love, health, curiosity, and learning.
- Be present in ordinary moments.
- Create routines for reading, games, experiments, and reflection.
- Support [[family/Nysha|Nysha]] and [[family/Navya|Navya]] with foundations, curiosity, emotional ability, and [[Confidence|confidence]].

## AI

- Become a deeply capable AI builder and domain expert.
- Build practical AI systems for work, family, and parenting.
- Learn daily.
- Teach or write to clarify thinking.

## Work And Leadership

- Lead with purpose, people, and process.
- Be direct.
- Say the hard thing.
- Build trust and high standards.
- Optimize for impact, not rating or title.

## Personal Operating System

- Practice calm response over reaction.
- Make faster decisions.
- Choose discomfort.
- Journal and reflect.
- Replace distraction with attention.
