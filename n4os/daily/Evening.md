# Evening

Use this to close the loop without judgment.

## Review

- Did I spend time on my highest priorities?
- Did I become a better father?
- Did I become healthier?
- Did I learn or build?
- Did I protect attention?
- Did I respond instead of react?

## Signal

- What gave me energy?
- What drained me?
- What triggered me?
- What fear showed up?
- What did I avoid?

## Gratitude

- What am I grateful for?
- Where did I see progress?
- What moment with family mattered?

## Adjustment

- What should I stop, replace, or simplify?
- What is tomorrow's highest leverage action?
