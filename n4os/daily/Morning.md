# Morning

Use this to start the day with alignment.

## Check In

- How is my energy?
- How is my body?
- How is my mind?
- What emotion is already driving the day?

## Priority

- What matters most today?
- Who needs me today?
- What is the highest leverage action?
- What can be ignored or delayed?

## Health

- How will I move today?
- What will protect sleep tonight?
- What is one food, mobility, or recovery choice that helps future me?

## Family

- How will I be present with Nysha, Navya, and family today?
- What moment should not be sacrificed for work?

## Purpose

- What will I learn, build, or decide that compounds?
