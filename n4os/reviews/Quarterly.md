# Quarterly Review

Use this to make strategic adjustments.

## Ten-Year Check

- Are Nysha and Navya getting the foundations they need?
- Am I building the health I want after fifty?
- Am I becoming a stronger AI product leader and builder?
- Am I investing deeply enough in family and friendships?

## Tradeoffs

- What did I sacrifice this quarter?
- Was that sacrifice aligned?
- What should no longer be sacrificed?

## Bets

- Which bet deserves more conviction?
- Which bet is incremental and should be replaced?
- Where am I playing too small?
- Where am I confusing motion with compounding?

## Reset

- What is the next quarter's theme?
- What are the three most important outcomes?
- What routines must become boring and reliable?
- What uncomfortable move must happen?
