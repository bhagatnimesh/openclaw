# Monthly Review

Use this to check direction, not just activity.

## Alignment

- Did my calendar reflect health, family, purpose, relationships, and learning?
- What lower priority quietly displaced a higher one?
- What am I optimizing for that I claim is not the goal?

## Progress

- What improved in health?
- What improved in parenting and family presence?
- What improved in AI mastery or product leadership?
- What relationship became stronger?
- What system now makes life easier?

## Decisions

- What decision did I delay?
- What should I quit, replace, or simplify?
- What big bet deserves more time, money, or attention?
- What fear is over-influencing me?
