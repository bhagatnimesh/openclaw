# Decision Filter

Use this whenever a decision feels important, noisy, emotional, or stuck.

## 1. Name The Decision

What exactly must be decided?

What decision would free attention?

## 2. Apply Priority Order

Does this support or harm:

1. health
2. family
3. purpose
4. relationships
5. learning
6. work
7. money
8. recognition

If lower priorities are winning over higher ones, correct the decision.

## 3. Check The Fear

Is the decision being driven by:

- fear of failure
- fear of judgment
- fear of uncertainty
- fear of lost opportunity
- fear of running out of time
- fear of discomfort
- fear of financial insecurity
- fear of not reaching potential

Fear is information, not instruction.

## 4. Check The Upside

If downside is non-catastrophic, optimize for peak upside, learning, and compounding.

Avoid loss aversion disguised as wisdom.

## 5. Check Time Horizon

Will I respect this decision in ten years?

Does it build the person, family, health, relationships, or AI mastery I want?

## 6. Decide

Choose:

- the decision
- the tradeoff
- the first action
- the review date
