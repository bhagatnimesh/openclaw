# AI Playbook

## Objective

Become one of the world's strongest AI product leaders and builders.

Do not merely work in AI.

Help people, organizations, and families transform through AI.

## Operating Mode

- Learn daily.
- Build practical systems.
- Teach to clarify.
- Stay close to users.
- Break assumptions.
- Prefer working prototypes over theoretical learning.
- Look for 10x and 100x opportunities.
- Build AI for purpose, not novelty.

## Focus

- AI-native developer experience.
- AI for parenting and family life.
- Product leadership in the AI era.
- Systems that help people learn, work, and live better.

## Trap

Do not confuse reading about AI with becoming an AI builder.

Build.
