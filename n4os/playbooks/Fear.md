# Fear Playbook

## Rule

Fear is information, not instruction.

## Name The Fear

- failure
- judgment
- uncertainty
- lost opportunity
- running out of time
- discomfort
- money insecurity
- family or health risk
- relevance after fifty
- not reaching potential

## Response

1. Name the fear.
2. Separate real risk from imagined permanence.
3. Ask what is in my control.
4. Check whether downside is catastrophic or survivable.
5. If survivable, move toward learning and upside.
6. Take one concrete action.

## Reminder

Failure, dislike, and being misunderstood are features of innovation and differentiation, not bugs.
