# Relationships Playbook

## Objective

Build lifelong family and friend relationships through presence, trust, support, and positive-sum action.

## Rules

- Be available.
- Know what is happening.
- Help people succeed.
- Build bridges with challenging people when it serves purpose.
- Have hard conversations early.
- Do not gossip or blame.
- Leave people stronger.
- Choose kindness without becoming approval-seeking.

## Review

- Who needs me now?
- Who have I not checked in on?
- Where should I repair, thank, or support?
- Which relationship compounds if I invest this week?
