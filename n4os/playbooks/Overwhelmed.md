# Overwhelmed Playbook

## First Move

Slow down.

You cannot do everything, and definitely not today.

## Process

1. Brain dump everything.
2. Mark what serves health, family, purpose, relationships, or learning.
3. Choose the highest leverage item.
4. Define the smallest meaningful next action.
5. Do it for a fixed block.
6. Reassess.

## Questions

- What is real and urgent?
- What is just noise?
- What can be delayed?
- What can be deleted?
- What decision would free attention?
- What would I choose if I trusted things would work out?

## Reminder

When overwhelmed, make a choice on the most important thing and trust.
