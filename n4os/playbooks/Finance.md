# Finance Playbook

## Objective

Use money to create freedom of choice.

Money is not the measure of success.

## Rules

- Do not optimize life around maximum compensation.
- Do not lose sleep over potential profit.
- Do not let money fear block 100x upside when downside is survivable.
- Track enough to reduce anxiety and make good decisions.
- Build freedom, not identity.

## Decision Check

- Does this financial decision protect family and health?
- Does it buy freedom or status?
- Is fear exaggerating downside?
- Is greed exaggerating upside?
- What is the simple next responsible action?
