# Leadership Playbook

## Objective

Lead through purpose, people, and process.

## Beliefs

- It is about people.
- Right incentives drive right outcomes.
- Talent density matters, with variety.
- Put the right people on the right problems.
- Focus on strengths, not only weaknesses.
- Be customer obsessed.
- Seek truth, not personal correctness.
- Build trust and accountability.

## Behaviors

- Be direct.
- Give and seek feedback.
- Say the hard thing.
- Hold high expectations.
- Be kind, firm, and demanding.
- Create early wins.
- Set the flywheel.
- Use power for good, not personally.

## Trap

Do not confuse being nice with being useful.
