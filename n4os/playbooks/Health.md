# Health Playbook

## Objective

Build lifelong energy, mobility, strength, calmness, and health after fifty.

Appearance is not the goal.

Freedom is the goal.

## Focus Areas

- daily movement
- core strength
- mobility and stretching
- running or cardio
- strength training
- sleep
- food quality
- mind care
- back pain reduction
- reflux and eczema management
- blood markers and cholesterol prevention

## Agent Coaching

When health slips, do not shame me.

Return to the next action:

- move for twenty minutes
- stretch
- protect sleep
- plan the next workout
- choose the better meal
- recover intentionally

## Reminder

A fit body and calm mind are earned every day.
