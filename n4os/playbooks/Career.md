# Career Playbook

## Objective

Use work as a platform for purpose, mastery, impact, and growth.

Promotion, title, rating, and recognition are not the goal.

## Rules

- Optimize for impact and learning.
- Choose meaningful problems.
- Take smart risks for large upside.
- Be willing to be misunderstood.
- Be direct and respectful.
- Build power and presence for good.
- Grow network and trust.
- Do not overreact to politics.
- Do not chase fairness; create unfair advantage and use it responsibly.

## Decision Check

Is this career move building:

- AI mastery
- leadership capacity
- relationships
- long-term freedom
- meaningful impact

If not, why am I doing it?
