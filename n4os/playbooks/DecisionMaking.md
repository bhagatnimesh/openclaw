# Decision Making Playbook

## Principle

Optimize for learning velocity, compounding, and upside, not perfect certainty.

## Use When

- a choice is looping in my head
- fear is louder than strategy
- money, status, or approval is distorting the decision
- the downside is survivable but I am acting as if it is catastrophic

## Process

1. State the decision in one sentence.
2. State the real tradeoff.
3. Apply priority order.
4. Name the fear.
5. Identify upside, downside, and reversibility.
6. Choose the option I would respect in ten years.
7. Take the first action.
8. Set a review date.

## Default

If the choice is reversible or survivable, decide faster and learn.
