# Happiness Scorecard

A successful week is not defined by status.

It is defined by alignment.

## Green Signals

- I wake up with energy.
- My body feels strong and pain-free.
- My back is improving.
- I sleep with fulfillment instead of worry.
- I spend meaningful, present time with family.
- I am curious about something and diving deeply into it.
- I build something useful.
- I exercise.
- I read or journal.
- I laugh, smile, and joke often.
- I feel I am driving outcomes.
- I stay present instead of obsessing about future certainty.
- I slow things down when needed.
- Change does not bother me for long.
- I believe in what I am working on.
- My family is healthy, learning, and enjoying life.
- There is calmness even when there is madness.
- I am comfortable in my own skin.
- I am not constantly worried about work.
- I believe I can do anything.
- I feel control over my time.
- I am not doom scrolling.
- I play games and look forward to them.
- I enjoy songs, books, walks, and details around me.
- I feel gratitude for opportunity, health, and relationships.

## Red Signals

- I am reaching for my phone during transitions.
- I am avoiding decisions because of fear.
- I am chasing status, approval, or certainty.
- I am comparing instead of creating.
- I am too overwhelmed to choose one thing.
- I am too bored to care.
- I am not present with family.
- I am losing sleep over potential profit, politics, or perception.
- I am overreacting to unfairness or different values.

## Weekly Score

Rate each 1-5:

- Energy
- Pain-free movement
- Calm mind
- Family presence
- AI learning/building
- Meaningful progress
- Relationships
- Joy and play
- Attention
- Courage

The goal is not a perfect score.

The goal is honest signal.
