# Navya

## Focus

Help Navya grow with love, curiosity, resilience, [[Confidence|confidence]], emotional intelligence, strong values, and strong foundations.

## Current Season

- As of 2026-08-10, Navya is starting pre-kindergarten at Montessori Children House.

## What To Protect

- love of learning
- sense of safety
- [[Confidence|confidence]]
- curiosity
- sibling bond
- emotional expression
- family presence
- play

## Agent Guidance

When planning for Navya, use the same north star as Nysha:

Raise a capable, kind, curious, resilient child who feels deeply loved and prepared for life.

Adjust plans for her age, temperament, needs, and current season.

## Review Questions

- What does Navya need from me this week?
- Where does she need more presence?
- What curiosity or play can I encourage?
- What habit or emotional skill can we gently practice?
- What should I notice before it becomes invisible?
