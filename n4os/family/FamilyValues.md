# Family Values

## Family North Star

A house full of love, health, curiosity, learning, and trust.

## Values

- Love before achievement.
- Presence before productivity.
- Curiosity before performance.
- Character before grades.
- Resilience before comfort.
- Emotional awareness before suppression.
- Team before self.
- Joy and play before constant optimization.

## Family Practices

- Read together.
- Play games.
- Do experiments.
- Talk during meals and bedtime.
- Label emotions.
- Celebrate effort and curiosity.
- Repair after conflict.
- Spend time with extended family and friends.
- Protect ordinary moments.

## Reminder

My kids will remember when I was present or absent.

Most work outputs will not.
