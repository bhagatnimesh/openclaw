# Operating Rules

## Default Loop

1. Gather the facts.
2. Name the fear, tradeoff, or constraint.
3. Choose the highest leverage action.
4. Act before certainty.
5. Reflect and adjust.

## Situational Rules

- When overwhelmed: choose one highest leverage task, finish it, repeat.
- When afraid: identify the fear, then move one step toward discomfort.
- When impatient: remember compounding is invisible until it is not.
- When distracted: protect attention; attention is everything.
- When work becomes political: optimize for purpose, not validation.
- When uncertain: decide quickly enough to create learning.
- When exhausted: recover intentionally; recovery is part of performance.
- When reactive: slow down, breathe, respond.
- When tempted by status: purpose over prestige.
- When bored: find a harder, more meaningful problem or create one.
- When comparing: return to my own definition of success.
- When something stays on my mind: make a decision and free attention.
- When a goal feels unreasonable: check downside, then play bigger.

## Daily Rules

- Move my body.
- Protect sleep.
- Spend present time with family.
- Learn something about AI or product.
- Build or improve one useful thing.
- Read or reflect.
- Say no to low-leverage distraction.

## Weekly Rules

- Leave behind one compounding asset.
- Review health, family, AI progress, relationships, and attention.
- Replace what is not working; do not merely stop it.
- Make one uncomfortable but important move.
